Banking support

Banking support that continues after the first decline.

Opening a U.S. business account as a non-resident is where many founders get stuck — not at formation. We guide you through the application process and help identify banking options suited to your country and business instead of sending everyone to the same place. If an application is declined, we don’t disappear: we review the available feedback and help you evaluate another eligible option included in your service plan.

Approval is always the financial institution’s decision — we prepare your documents, guide the application, and stay with you through it. We don’t make promises a bank hasn’t made.

CrossPoint is not a bank, law firm, or tax advisor, and does not guarantee account approval. Eligibility, processing time, and final decisions are determined by each financial institution.

Questions

Non-resident company formation FAQs.

Answers to common questions about non-resident company formation, banking documentation, director requirements, and government fees.

Can I open a company if I do not live in Canada or the U.S.?

Yes, many Canada and U.S. company setup paths are available to non-residents. Eligibility depends on your country of residence, business activity and documentation.

Which is better for me: Canada or the U.S.?

It depends on your business goal, customers, banking needs, payment platforms, and tax and compliance situation. We walk you through the comparison before you pay anything.

Do you guarantee a bank account?

No. CrossPoint is not a bank and does not guarantee approval. We provide banking documentation guidance and help prepare your application, but every institution makes its own decision.

What documents will I need?

Usually government ID such as a passport, contact details, business activity details, and formation details such as the company name and structure.

How long does the process usually take?

Formation timing depends on the country, state, province and government processing. Banking and payment platform steps come after formation and take longer.

Do I need a resident director for a Canadian corporation?

It depends on the jurisdiction. Some Canadian jurisdictions allow fully non-resident directors; others require resident directors. We confirm the rule for your chosen jurisdiction before filing.

Can I use my company for Amazon, Shopify, or Stripe?

Many non-residents form a company specifically for these platforms. Each platform sets its own requirements and makes its own approval decision.

Are government fees included?

Government filing fees are charged separately at cost and confirmed before payment. This applies to Canadian government filing fees, U.S. state fees and other third-party costs.
